What is the Exchange?
The CVC Exchange lets students cross-enroll in fully online courses at other California community colleges through a single portal. When enrollment data flows correctly from a home college (Banner, PeopleSoft, Colleague) through the Exchange to a teaching college and into Canvas — the student never notices. When it breaks, they lose access to their course. Our job is to keep it quiet.

Subject: Your MATH 1A enrollment — update
We're aware your MATH 1A section isn't showing in Canvas yet. This is a sync issue between your home college and the teaching college — not a problem with your enrollment. Why this tone Your seat is secure. We expect this to be resolved within 2–4 hours. No action needed on your end.

To Campus IT (Teaching College)
ServiceNow
Enrollment record for student ID 0012847593 was sent from Chabot at 14:22 PST but the Canvas roster for MATH-1A-001 doesn't reflect it yet. The connection credential at the home college returned an access denied error. Can you verify whether the enrollment record exists in your student system? Why a question
📋
To the Registrar
Email
Subject: Cross-enrollment sync — student enrollments confirmed, Canvas delay expected <4hr
Quick update on the cross-enrollment sync issue. The student's enrollment is confirmed — the delay is in how the data reaches Canvas. Why this framing If students contact your office, you can confirm their enrollment is valid. Happy to provide a written summary if this needs to go to your VP.
⚙
To CVC Internal Team
Slack
Scope: 47 student enrollments pending in Chabot → De Anza queue since 02:00 PST. Root cause: security credential expired, access denied on renewal attempt. Action: Credential regenerated. Queue processing in progress. Knowledge base article drafted.
📊
Board Brief (via Vice Chancellor)
Quarterly ETS Report
Situation: During Fall 2025 peak registration, an authentication failure between the CVC Exchange and 7 Banner colleges caused enrollment sync delays affecting approximately 340 students across 2 consecutive terms.

Student Impact: 47 students experienced 4–12 hour delays in Canvas course access. 12 required manual intervention near the add deadline. Zero students lost enrollment; all resolved within SLA.

Equity note: Disproportionate impact on low-income students — the sync delay coincided with auto-drop-for-nonpayment windows at 3 colleges.

Root cause (plain language): An expired security credential between two systems. Analogous to a building key card that stopped working — the door is fine, the person is authorized, but the reader rejects them until IT reissues the credential. Why an analogy

Remediation: Credential regenerated. Automated expiration monitoring added (alerts 72 hours before expiration). KB article published.

Recommendation: No board action required. Status update recommended in quarterly ETS report under “CVC Exchange Reliability.”

P1 Critical
What it looks like
Multiple colleges down. Enrollment queue completely blocked. Students cannot enroll or access Canvas across 2+ institutions.
Response time
Immediate. Drop everything.
Who to contact
Senior analyst + supervisor simultaneously. If Ethos/Ellucian: vendor support with case number. Campus IT leads at affected colleges.
What you need first
Which colleges. How many students (check monitor). Error codes from tracer. When it started (check logs).
Comms
All 4 audiences within 30 minutes. Student-facing message first.
Root Layer
Vendor Campus IT CVC Infrastructure
Multi-college failures are usually vendor or CVC infrastructure. Name the layer before assigning blame.
P2 Degraded
What it looks like
One college degraded. Sync delays, partial failures, seat count mismatches. Students can enroll but may see stale data.
Response time
Within 2 hours. Triage, diagnose, then escalate if needed.
Who to contact
Campus IT at the affected college. Senior analyst if root cause is unclear after 30 min of investigation.
What you need first
SIS type. Specific error from tracer. Whether it affects enrollment, sync, or Canvas only. Approximate student count.
Comms
Internal Slack update. Campus IT email with diagnostic detail. Student notice only if enrollment is blocked.
Root Layer
Campus IT Vendor (credential)
CVC's role is to diagnose and coordinate, not to own the fix.
P3 Minor / Known
What it looks like
Individual student issue or a known limitation. Fee waiver (CCPG) not transferring, Canvas roster delay, DSPS accommodation gap.
Response time
Within 1 business day. Standard queue priority.
Who to contact
Handle directly. Escalate to supervisor only if the student has a deadline within 48 hours that will be missed.
What you need first
Student ID (CCCID). Home college + teaching college. Specific error or symptom. Has the student already contacted either college?
Comms
Direct student response. KB article link if one exists. Document the resolution for pattern tracking.
Root Layer
Student Action System Gap
P3 tickets are often not failures — they're design gaps. Document the pattern; don't apologize for the architecture.

Student & Counselor Toolkit

▸ For counselors and staff
🔍
It's not just GE
The CVC Exchange includes transfer-applicable courses, major prep courses, and courses with C-ID designations that are accepted system-wide.
✗
Myth: “CVC is only for GE breadth courses.”
✓
Reality: Any fully online course at a participating college can be listed. This includes major prep courses, courses with C-ID numbers, and Cal-GETC Area 6 Ethnic Studies courses added Fall 2025.
✗
Myth: “If Foothill doesn't offer PSYCH 1A this term, the student is stuck.”
✓
Reality: Go to search.cvc.edu, enter “Foothill College” as home college, select “Home College Course Name,” type PSYCH 1A. The Exchange will show equivalent sections at other colleges with live seat counts.
✅
3-step verification workflow
Before recommending an Exchange course, counselors can verify transferability in under 2 minutes using three free tools.
1
CVC search — Go to search.cvc.edu. Select student's home college. Search by Cal-GETC area, CSU GE area, or Home College Course Name. Confirm the course has seats and is marked “Add Class.”
2
ASSIST check — Go to assist.org. Select the teaching college and the student's transfer destination. Verify the course appears in the articulation agreement.
3
C-ID confirm — If the course has a C-ID number (e.g., C-ID COMM 130), it means California colleges have determined this course is comparable. Check c-id.net
Time to verify: Under 2 minutes for a counselor who has done it once.
#1 PREVENTION
📋
Student registration checklist
Most Exchange support tickets trace back to a registration step the student didn't know about. These 7 steps prevent the ticket from ever being created.
7 steps before the student clicks “Add Course”
Confirm you're enrolled at your home college this term in at least one course.
Search at search.cvc.edu — set your home college first. Only click courses marked “Add Class.”
Verify with your counselor that this specific course satisfies your requirement.
Pay immediately or set up financial aid. Teaching colleges may drop you for nonpayment.
⚠ Timing gap: FA Consortium Agreements take 3–5 business days. Some teaching colleges drop within 48 hours. Pay now, get reimbursed later.
Consent to transcript sharing during enrollment.
Check Canvas within 24 hours. Log in using your home college credentials. If the course doesn't appear, contact CVC support.
Know the 2-course limit. Max 2 Exchange courses per term. Winter intersession counts toward spring.
💬
What to say to the student
Sample counselor language that sets accurate expectations. Tested against common misunderstandings.
“You need HIST 17A for your CSU transfer, and it's not offered here this term.”
“I'm going to search the CVC Exchange for an equivalent. Let me check ASSIST to make sure it counts at [target CSU]. Here — Chabot College offers it online, it has a C-ID match, and there are 8 seats left.”
“I want to take an extra class but everything's full.”
“The Exchange has courses from over 100 colleges. Remember, up to 2 Exchange courses per term, same cost — $46 per unit. Your fee waiver doesn't auto-transfer though.”
“Is this the same as taking a class at another college?”
“Yes, but simpler. You don't fill out a new application — the Exchange handles that. You log into Canvas with your same home college credentials. Your transcript transfers automatically.”
▸ For students (click to expand)
🎓
Will this course count toward my degree?
You can verify yourself in under 2 minutes. Here's how to confirm any Exchange course will transfer.
1
Go to assist.org — Select the college offering the Exchange course, then select where you want to transfer. If the course appears as articulated, it counts.
2
Check for a C-ID number — If it has one, California colleges have agreed this course is equivalent statewide.
3
Look for badges on search.cvc.edu — “Quality Reviewed” means it passed CVC-OEI peer review. “Zero Textbook Cost” uses free OER.
Still unsure? Bring the course name, teaching college, and C-ID number to your counselor.
💰
How do I pay? Will my financial aid cover it?
Three payment paths — and the biggest mistake students make is waiting.
💳
Credit card (fastest): Pay at enrollment. If you're later approved for aid, you'll be reimbursed.
🏷
CCPG fee waiver: Must apply separately at the teaching college. It does not transfer from your home college.
📋
Consortium Agreement: Contact your home college FA office. Takes 3–5 business days. Start before registration.
⚠ The #1 mistake: Enrolling, waiting for FA paperwork, and getting auto-dropped for nonpayment within 48 hours.
▸ For financial aid offices (click to expand)
Since April 2025: The CVC's centralized Financial Aid Dashboard automates unit recording for Banner Direct and PeopleSoft colleges. Ethos colleges are pending. For colleges already on the dashboard, the manual email workflow for Consortium Agreements is being replaced. FA offices should confirm their college's integration status and update internal procedures accordingly.
