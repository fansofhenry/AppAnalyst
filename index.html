The Problem
A student at Chabot enrolls in MATH 1A at De Anza through the Exchange. The next morning, the course isn’t in Canvas. She doesn’t know why. She emails her counselor, who doesn’t know either. She considers dropping.
The cause was technical — an expired authentication token between two systems she’s never heard of. The support analyst’s job is to find it, fix it, tell the right people, and make sure it doesn’t happen to the next 46 students in the queue.

What 2,100% growth means for the support team
More colleges onboarding. From 16 to 78 teaching colleges — each with different SIS configurations, payment deadlines, and FA workflows. Every new college is a new integration to monitor.
More cross-system failures. 33K enrollments means 33K data flows through the AER, Ethos/Direct Application Programming Interfaces (APIs), Canvas provisioning, and FA Consortium pipeline. Failure rate stays constant; volume multiplies.
More students at risk. Every broken enrollment is a student who may miss a transfer deadline or lose financial aid. The support function scales or students fall through the cracks.

05Escalation

How do you know when to own it vs escalate it?

P1, P2, or P3? Who to contact, how fast, and what to bring.

Reference
Escalation Decision Matrix · P1 / P2 / P3
Current scenario: Sacramento City Ethos token expired — 1 college degraded, 47 students affected. Based on the evidence: this is a P2.
P1 Critical
What it looks like
Multiple colleges down. AER queue completely blocked. Students cannot enroll or access Canvas across 2+ institutions.
Response time
Immediate. Drop everything.
Who to contact
Senior analyst + supervisor simultaneously. If Ethos/Ellucian: vendor support with case number. Campus IT leads at affected colleges.
What you need first
Which colleges. How many students (check monitor). Error codes from tracer. When it started (check logs).
Comms
All 4 audiences within 30 minutes. Student-facing message first.
P2 DegradedCurrent
What it looks like
One college degraded. Sync delays, partial failures, seat count mismatches. Students can enroll but may see stale data.
Response time
Within 2 hours. Triage, diagnose, then escalate if needed.
Who to contact
Campus IT at the affected college. Senior analyst if root cause is unclear after 30 min of investigation.
What you need first
SIS type. Specific error from tracer. Whether it affects enrollment, sync, or Canvas only. Approximate student count.
Comms
Internal Slack update. Campus IT email with diagnostic detail. Student notice only if enrollment is blocked.
P3 Minor / Known
What it looks like
Individual student issue or a known limitation. CCPG not transferring, Canvas roster delay, Disabled Students Programs and Services (DSPS) accommodation gap.
Response time
Within 1 business day. Standard queue priority.
Who to contact
Handle directly. Escalate to supervisor only if the student has a deadline within 48 hours that will be missed.
What you need first
CCCID. Home college + teaching college. Specific error or symptom. Has the student already contacted either college?
Comms
Direct student response. KB article link if one exists. Document the resolution for pattern tracking.
The difference between P1 and P3 isn't just speed — it's who you contact and what information you bring to the call

11Counselor Toolkit

The Exchange is not just GE — and here's how to verify it

Counselors are the gateway. If they're uncertain about transfer credit, students never hear about the Exchange.

Reference
Counselor Confidence Guide · Course Verification · Registration Checklist
Why this matters: Some counselors think the CVC Exchange is limited to general education courses. In reality, students can search by CSU GE Breadth, Cal-GETC (replaces IGETC starting Fall 2025), substitute course by home college course name, major preparation courses via C-ID, and keyword. The verification tools already exist — counselors just need a workflow.
▸ Four things counselors need to know
🔍
It's not just GE
The CVC Exchange includes transfer-applicable courses, major prep courses, and courses with C-ID designations that are accepted system-wide. Students can search by Cal-GETC area, CSU GE area, or substitute their home college course by name.
✗
Myth: "CVC is only for GE breadth courses."
✓
Reality: Any fully online course at a participating college can be listed. This includes major prep courses (e.g., Intro to Business, Statistics for Social Sciences, Anatomy), courses with C-ID numbers that map across colleges and into the CSU system, and Cal-GETC Area 6 Ethnic Studies courses added Fall 2025.
✗
Myth: "If Foothill doesn't offer PSYCH 1A this term, the student is stuck."
✓
Reality: Go to search.cvc.edu, enter "Foothill College" as home college, select "Home College Course Name," type PSYCH 1A. The Exchange will show equivalent sections at other colleges with live seat counts.
✅
3-step verification workflow
Before recommending an Exchange course, counselors can verify transferability in under 2 minutes using three free tools. This is the workflow that builds confidence.
1
CVC search — Go to search.cvc.edu Select student's home college. Search by Cal-GETC area, CSU GE area, or Home College Course Name. Confirm the course has seats and is marked "Add Class" (instant enrollment).
2
ASSIST check — Go to assist.org Select the teaching college (where the Exchange course is offered) and the student's transfer destination (CSU or UC campus). Verify the course appears in the articulation agreement for the student's major or GE pattern.
3
C-ID confirm — If the course has a C-ID number (e.g., C-ID COMM 130), it means California colleges have determined this course is comparable in content and scope. A C-ID match is the strongest equivalency signal. Check c-id.net
Time to verify: Under 2 minutes for a counselor who has done it once. Under 30 seconds once the workflow is familiar. The tools are free, public, and already in use by articulation officers statewide.
📋
Student registration checklist
Most support tickets come from students who registered wrong. A counselor who walks through these 7 steps prevents the ticket from ever being created.
Print-ready — give this to the student before they register
Confirm you're enrolled at your home college this term in at least one course. You cannot use the Exchange if you're not currently enrolled.
Search at search.cvc.edu — set your home college first. Search by GE area or course name. Only click courses marked "Add Class" for instant enrollment.
Verify with your counselor that this specific course at this specific college satisfies your requirement. Bring the course name, college, and C-ID number if listed.
Pay immediately or set up financial aid. Teaching colleges may drop you for nonpayment. Three payment paths: credit card now, apply for CCPG at the teaching college (it does not transfer from your home college), or coordinate a Consortium Agreement through your home college's financial aid office.
Consent to transcript sharing during enrollment. This allows your grades to automatically transfer back to your home college at end of term.
Check Canvas within 24 hours. Log in using your home college credentials. Your Exchange course should appear alongside your other courses. If it doesn't, contact CVC support.
Know the 2-course limit. You can cross-enroll in a maximum of 2 Exchange courses per term. Winter intersession counts toward the spring term limit.
⚠ Common mistake that creates tickets: Student enrolls, doesn't pay, gets dropped for nonpayment, then panics. Step 4 prevents this. If the student is using financial aid, the Consortium Agreement can take days — they need to start that process before registration, not after.
💬
What to say to the student
Sample counselor language that sets accurate expectations and prevents confusion. Tested against the most common misunderstandings.
"You need HIST 17A for your CSU transfer, and it's not offered here this term."
"I'm going to search the CVC Exchange for an equivalent. Let me check ASSIST to make sure it counts at [target CSU]. Here — Chabot College offers it online, it has a C-ID match, and there are 8 seats left. If you enroll today, pay right away or talk to financial aid about a Consortium Agreement. The course will show up in your Canvas by tomorrow."
"I want to take an extra class but everything's full."
"The Exchange has courses from over 100 colleges. Let me search by your course name so we find an exact equivalent. Remember, you can take up to 2 Exchange courses per term, and they're the same cost — $46 per unit. Your fee waiver doesn't auto-transfer though, so you'd need to apply for CCPG at the teaching college separately."
"Is this the same as taking a class at another college?"
"Yes, but simpler. You don't fill out a new application — the Exchange handles that. You log into Canvas with your same home college credentials and the class appears alongside your other courses. Your transcript transfers automatically at end of term. The main thing to know: pay for the class right away, and if you need accommodations, you'll need to contact the teaching college's disability services separately."
